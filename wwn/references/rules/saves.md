# Saving Throws & Hazards

> Use this when: a hero tries to dodge a dire fate the hit roll doesn't cover — poison, a spell, a falling ceiling, a pit, privation, suffocation.

**Roll `dice.py roll 1d20`; success = result ≥ the save target. Higher rolls are better.**

## The four PC saves (target = 16 − level − attr mod)
- **Physical** — best of **Str/Con**. Poison, disease, exhaustion, bodily transformation.
- **Evasion** — best of **Int/Dex**. Hurled perils, blasts, sudden pits, reaction-speed dangers.
- **Mental** — best of **Wis/Cha**. Mind magic, illusions, intangible afflictions, willpower.
- **Luck** — **no attribute**, target = 16 − level. Blind fortune, when nothing about the hero could matter.

*Example:* a 3rd-level hero, Int +1 / Dex −1 → Evasion 16 − 3 − 1 = **12**.

**Monsters/NPCs** use one save = **15 − ½ HD** (round down). 1 HD → 15+; 14 HD → 8+.

Saves are **last-ditch** chances — not a substitute for combat (no Evasion save vs a sword stroke). The GM decides if a danger even allows a save.

## Common hazards
- **Poison (needle-trap grade):** Physical save or lose **½ max HP**; potent ones can kill outright (death over 1d6 min, helpless; antidote revives at 1 HP). Many add lingering penalties or System Strain. A healer may counter with Int/Heal vs diff 10 (12+ for fearsome toxins).
- **Disease:** Physical save to avoid infection; if it takes hold, weekly saves — N successes throw it off, repeated failures can kill (3–4 failed → fatal). Heal skill grants +2×level to these saves.
- **Falling:** **1d6 per full 10 ft** (max 20d6); spikes +1d6. A controlled leap = Dex or Str/Exert vs **7 + 1 per 10 ft**; success halves the effective distance.
- **Suffocation:** act 1 round per point of Con (×4 if motionless); then Physical save each round or take **1 HP per HD/level**.
