# Abhishek "Shake" Rao

> A player character's full record. Extends the engine's character record with WWN fields. A transported New Yorker — PGY-4 vascular surgery chief resident at NYU — whose medical art woke into healing sorcery on the crossing into Auragne.

- **Level / Class:** 1 · **Adventurer (Partial Warrior / Partial Healer)** — HD 1d6+2, attack +1, partial Healer tradition (Arts via Effort, works in armor)
- **Background:** Physician (NYC: vascular-surgery chief resident; Tisch / Bellevue / the VA)
- **Goal:** Stay alive, find his brother **Akhil** and his scattered people, and learn whether the rift can be recrossed — saving who he can with blade and Art along the way.
- **Ties:** A healer is wanted everywhere; he trades care for protection, and stands with fellow New Yorkers and anyone who keeps the group breathing.

## Who he is (New York)
Indian-American, 30, 5'11"/140, slim. From Newark, Delaware; parents both surgeons (Bhaskar — inventive, scattered; Sudha — grounded, retired). Younger brother **Akhil**, med student at Mount Sinai — the tie he'd miss most (and a fellow New Yorker: *was he taken too?*). Calm, dry-funny, practical, emotionally contained; blunt when task-focused; flexible under pressure, but loop-closing can slip. Heavy reader; action-RPGs and military history; lapsed Tae Kwon Do.
