# Setting Canon — Latter Earth / the Gyre   (ground truth; overrides recollection)

> **This is the LIVE canon a campaign starts from — the default.** A fresh world (worldgen, "generate a fresh setting") **replaces this whole file**. Entries here outrank model memory. Default starting region: **the Gyre**.

## Premise / tone
The **Latter Earth** is the unimaginably far future — a world so old that countless godlike empires lie buried beneath its dust, and the very laws of reality (the **Legacy**) are decaying. There is no progress, no science, no rescue: only the dangerous present and the lethal, treasure-laden past. Gritty post-apocalyptic sword-and-sorcery; adventurers are recognized, disposable, and on their own. (The whole world is one **Iterum** — echoed reality — coded *108 Triumphant Void*; this lets you change anything without "breaking canon.")

## The Latter Earth in brief
- **The Legacy** — the world's decaying source-code. Physical law is conditional and special-cased; magic and Workings fail unpredictably over ages. Why nothing advances and wildernesses a day from a capital are full of horrors no army can clear.
- **The Deeps** — vast buried megastructures dug by the Outsiders; the classic dungeon. New ones surface constantly; cities sit atop older buried ones.
- **Outsiders** — the aliens who once ruled and enslaved humanity from the Deeps; now hunted remnants, genuinely **alien** in motive (Jikegida parasites, Polop sea-devils, Tuhulot "folded men"). They trapped themselves here forever and hate humanity.
- **Arratu** — xenoformed wastelands left by the Outsiders; alien atmospheres, mutant flora/fauna, well-preserved ruins. Can spread on their own.
- **Sorcerer-kings & Legates** — humans rose from the Deeps to cast down the Outsiders; their heirs founded the nations. Superhuman near-immortal rulers (in the Gyre, the **Reaping King**) wield power no commoner can resist.
- **The Blighted** — cursed human-descended peoples; the **Anakim** are warborn, hate-driven Blighted of the wastes.

## Powers & how they want / act
**The Gyre** (default start) — a fractured region once unified by the immortal **Reaping King**, now silent in folded Ka-Adun while six successor-states circle for hegemony amid Anak raids and a feared new war. Its six nations:
- **Emed-Kist** — loose monarchy; Great King Syet Lun balances grudge-ridden feudal lords against royal-loyal Darian merchant-commoners. *Wants:* unity, a reconquest of lost Emed-Dar. *Acts:* placate lords, lean on commoners, hire swords.
- **Ka-Adun** — vast dead megalopolis; the silent Reaping King, the Black Brass Legion building forever, Grand Censor Euphon's faction scrabbling in a too-large impoverished nobility. *Wants:* survival, tribute, the Hegemony's restoration. *Acts:* desperate vigilance; beggar-princes plunder each other.
- **Llaigis** — grim half-tamed land of warlords, mercenaries, and ruin-plunder; King Hulit of Gathis is first among many. *Wants:* silver, warriors, land. *Acts:* prove every claim by force; tolerate cults that prove useful.
- **New Voth** — the lone republic; senators (the rich) drive a gold-rush of arratu-settling and ruin-plunder; horror of mind-sorcery, slavery outlawed. *Wants:* restore the old Republic, expand. *Acts:* enrich the strong, give losers no mercy.
- **Sarul** — theocracy of the **Blighted**, ruled by the Church of the **Bleeding God**; brutal law, fearsome Anak crusaders. *Wants:* faith spread, the faithful saved, the Anak of Emed-Dar held off. *Acts:* merciless justice; fight the Inquisition's own corruption.
- **Thur** — fragile guild-industrial state running ancient **Workings**; the Gentry rule via Great Guilds and corrupt elite schools. *Wants:* keep the Workings (and their power) running. *Acts:* gatekeep, scheme in the schools; fear sabotage.
- **The wider Atlas** — beyond the Gyre lie the two continents of the West (Agathon & Gyarus): **Atlantis**, **Sarx**, **Ondas**, **Vitrum**, **Kytheron**, the **Amundi**, and dozens more. Mostly indifferent to the isolated Gyre — until it unites. Pointer: `book/The-Atlas-of-the-Latter-Earth/02-The-Lands-of-the-West/`.

## Key truths / tone
- No progress, no science, no cavalry; the world owes nothing and punishes passivity.
- Power is personal and brutal; rulers are pragmatic, sorcerers self-interested, commoners survival-bent, Outsiders alien.
- The past is real, ancient, mostly indifferent — and lethal to wake.
- Death, maiming, ruin, and exile are permanent (engine Creed).
