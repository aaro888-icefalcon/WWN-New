# Setting Canon — Latter Earth / the Gyre   (ground truth; overrides recollection)

> **This is the LIVE canon a campaign starts from — the default.** A fresh world (worldgen, "generate a fresh setting") **replaces this whole file**. Entries here outrank model memory. Default starting region **(this campaign)**: the **Kingdom of Auragne** — the Lost March of Amund, on the continent of Agathon (see **ACTIVE CAMPAIGN** below, which overrides the Gyre as the start). The Gyre material remains valid canon for the wider Latter Earth.

## Premise / tone
The **Latter Earth** is the unimaginably far future — a world so old that countless godlike empires lie buried beneath its dust, and the very laws of reality (the **Legacy**) are decaying. There is no progress, no science, no rescue: only the dangerous present and the lethal, treasure-laden past. Gritty post-apocalyptic sword-and-sorcery; adventurers are recognized, disposable, and on their own. (The whole world is one **Iterum** — echoed reality — coded *108 Triumphant Void*; this lets you change anything without "breaking canon.")

## The Latter Earth in brief
- **The Legacy** — the world's decaying source-code. Physical law is conditional and special-cased; magic and Workings fail unpredictably over ages. Why nothing advances and wildernesses a day from a capital are full of horrors no army can clear.
- **The Deeps** — vast buried megastructures dug by the Outsiders; the classic dungeon. New ones surface constantly; cities sit atop older buried ones.
- **Outsiders** — the aliens who once ruled and enslaved humanity from the Deeps; now hunted remnants, genuinely **alien** in motive (Jikegida parasites, Polop sea-devils, Tuhulot "folded men"). They trapped themselves here forever and hate humanity.
- **Arratu** — xenoformed wastelands left by the Outsiders; alien atmospheres, mutant flora/fauna, well-preserved ruins. Can spread on their own.
- **Sorcerer-kings & Legates** — humans rose from the Deeps to cast down the Outsiders; their heirs founded the nations. Superhuman near-immortal rulers (in the Gyre, the **Reaping King**) wield power no commoner can resist.
- **The Blighted** — cursed human-descended peoples; the **Anakim** are warborn, hate-driven Blighted of the wastes.

## Powers & how they want / act
**The Gyre** (default start) — a fractured region once unified by the immortal **Reaping King**, now silent in folded Ka-Adun while six successor-states circle for hegemony amid Anak raids and a feared new war. Its six nations:
- **Emed-Kist** — loose monarchy; Great King Syet Lun balances grudge-ridden feudal lords against royal-loyal Darian merchant-commoners. *Wants:* unity, a reconquest of lost Emed-Dar. *Acts:* placate lords, lean on commoners, hire swords.
- **Ka-Adun** — vast dead megalopolis; the silent Reaping King, the Black Brass Legion building forever, Grand Censor Euphon's faction scrabbling in a too-large impoverished nobility. *Wants:* survival, tribute, the Hegemony's restoration. *Acts:* desperate vigilance; beggar-princes plunder each other.
- **Llaigis** — grim half-tamed land of warlords, mercenaries, and ruin-plunder; King Hulit of Gathis is first among many. *Wants:* silver, warriors, land. *Acts:* prove every claim by force; tolerate cults that prove useful.
- **New Voth** — the lone republic; senators (the rich) drive a gold-rush of arratu-settling and ruin-plunder; horror of mind-sorcery, slavery outlawed. *Wants:* restore the old Republic, expand. *Acts:* enrich the strong, give losers no mercy.
- **Sarul** — theocracy of the **Blighted**, ruled by the Church of the **Bleeding God**; brutal law, fearsome Anak crusaders. *Wants:* faith spread, the faithful saved, the Anak of Emed-Dar held off. *Acts:* merciless justice; fight the Inquisition's own corruption.
- **Thur** — fragile guild-industrial state running ancient **Workings**; the Gentry rule via Great Guilds and corrupt elite schools. *Wants:* keep the Workings (and their power) running. *Acts:* gatekeep, scheme in the schools; fear sabotage.
- **The wider Atlas** — beyond the Gyre lie the two continents of the West (Agathon & Gyarus): **Atlantis**, **Sarx**, **Ondas**, **Vitrum**, **Kytheron**, the **Amundi**, and dozens more. Mostly indifferent to the isolated Gyre — until it unites. Pointer: `book/The-Atlas-of-the-Latter-Earth/02-The-Lands-of-the-West/`.

## Key truths / tone
- No progress, no science, no cavalry; the world owes nothing and punishes passivity.
- Power is personal and brutal; rulers are pragmatic, sorcerers self-interested, commoners survival-bent, Outsiders alien.
- The past is real, ancient, mostly indifferent — and lethal to wake.
- Death, maiming, ruin, and exile are permanent (engine Creed).

# ACTIVE CAMPAIGN — The Kingdom of Auragne (the Lost March of Amund)

> **Guided Latter Earth.** Auragne is the canonically-blank **Lost March** of the Amundi Kingdoms (Atlas: *"a creative storyteller could make up almost any kingdom for the March"*). Built here as a powerful, revanchist **Norse/Saxon Marchen kingdom** of the god-burnt north of Agathon — Carven Peaks to the west, pine forests, the **Marchen** people (canon: remnants of a much older kingdom that fell in the First Age). Machine graph in `campaign/places.json`; faction board in `campaign/factions.md`; Lists in `threads.json`/`characters.json`. **Chaos Factor floor 6** (active civil war / lawless frontier).

## The premise — the Arrival
Everyone from the modern city of **New York** was torn out of their world and scattered across the continent of **Agathon**. The largest shard fell through a **shattered First-Age Working** (the *Working Wreckage* of Old Vael) beside Auragne's capital and rained a slice of the city — and the **player-character** — into the **Shire of Monze**, months ago. The Latter Earth did not ask and does not care: tens of thousands of bewildered modern people now scrabble to survive a lethal sword-and-sorcery world mid–civil war, and every power covets them. **The PC is one of the fallen New Yorkers.**

## The kingdom & its crisis
Great **King Urcrin** united the seven shires and was about to **reconquer Old Vael** — the lost ancestral empire — when he abruptly **died** (*how he truly died is an open question, not yet earned*). The realm now cracks **three ways** for the **Dragon Throne**:

| Claimant | Who | Holds | Foreign backer | Faction (board) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Boy-King Phillipe** | dragon-touched child heir, true king | the legitimacy; little else | a faction of **the Arrival** | The Dragon Throne |
| **Lord Protector Almeric** | the dead king's brother, regent | the **capital Aurholt** and the boy himself | another faction of **the Arrival** | The Lord Protector's Party |
| **Countess Marie of Mauressac** | a count playing for the crown herself | guild wealth, a western power base | the **Shinbu Anak** pirates | The Mauressac Claim |

The Arrival (the New Yorkers) is **split** — rival diaspora factions prop up Phillipe and Almeric, making the PC's own people the pivotal kingmaker.

## Old Vael & the dragon Vörniss (the reconquest's true object)
**Old Vael** was the First-Age Marchen hegemony, founded and dominated by the dragon **Vörniss, the Crowned Worm**. It **fell by economic collapse** (its bound-sorcery wealth imploded); its heartland is now a western waste/arratu of opulent ruin. **Vörniss** — Legate-tier, **psychic breath**, drive of **Domination** — still dreams in its lair (**Vörniss' Crown**, ruin R-06), and its blood runs in Auragne's kings (the "dragon-touched"). The fanatic **Worm-Cult of Vael** works to **wake** it. Whoever wins the throne inherits the reconquest — and the Worm.

## The seven shires (each a region in `places.json`; count, tags, backer, stance, feuds, delve)
Stance = which claimant the count **opposes** (leans toward the other two). Enemies/ally are among the **other counts** (asymmetries are real — treachery).

1. **Altdobern** (RG-01) — *terrain:* Blighted Tribe · *seat:* Altdobern (Ancient Infrastructure) · *court:* Outside Debts. **Count Heinrich** · backer **Shinbu Anak** · opposes **Phillipe** · enemies Marie & Gruith · ally Qasim. Delve: **The Blighted Warren** (R-02).
2. **Verzeille** (RG-02) — *terrain:* Military Outpost · *seat:* Verzeille (New Industry) · *court:* Fatal Extravagance. **Countess Deidre** · backer **Mishar** · opposes **Marie** · enemies Qasim & Carloman · ally Gruith. Delve: **The Drowned Vault** (R-03).
3. **Hermsdorf** (RG-03) — *terrain:* Perilous Path · *seat:* Hermsdorf (Cursed Circumstances) · *court:* Decadent Court. **Count Carloman** · backer **Pelegrin** · opposes **Almeric** · enemies Heinrich & Chilperic · ally Deidre. Delve: **The Murkfen Working** (R-05).
4. **Mauressac** (RG-04) — *terrain:* Rampant Experiment · *seat:* Mauressac (Guild Oligarchy) · *court:* Cultural Insignia. **Countess Marie** *(throne-claimant)* · backer **Shinbu Anak** · enemies Deidre & Carloman · ally Gruith. Delves: **Vael-fane** (R-01) and, to her west, **Vörniss' Crown** (R-06).
5. **Briach** (RG-05) — *terrain:* Blighted Tribe · *seat:* Briach (Population Boom) · *court:* Outside Debts. **Count Gruith** · backer **Mishar** · opposes **Almeric** · enemies Qasim & Carloman · ally **Marie** *(mutual bloc)*.
6. **Shakal** (RG-06) — *terrain:* Overgrown Tomb · *seat:* Shakal (Upstart Faith) · *court:* Recent Brutality. **Count Qasim** *(foreign-blooded southern march)* · backer **Mishar** · opposes **Marie** · enemies Carloman & Deidre · ally Gruith. Delve: **The Hive-hold** (R-04).
7. **Monze** (RG-07) — *terrain:* **Working Wreckage = THE RIFT** · *seat:* **Aurholt** (royal capital; Seat of Rule) · *court:* Capricious Orders. **Count Chilperic** · backer **Nabardura** · opposes **Phillipe** · enemies Gruith & Qasim · ally Deidre. **The capital**, held by Almeric with the boy-king; the Arrival clusters at the rift here. **PC's starting ground.**

## Foreign backers (the meddler axis)
- **Mishar** (Despot **Amiya**, the warlike canon queen) is **buying up the realm** — backs **Deidre, Gruith, Qasim** (3 counts). Faction: **Mishar's Hand**, goal *Vassalize Auragne*.
- **Shinbu Anak** (pirate Blighted) — back **Marie** and **Heinrich**.
- **Pelegrin** — backs Carloman. **Nabardura** — backs Chilperic.
- **The Arrival** (New Yorkers) — split, backs **Phillipe** and **Almeric**.

## Faction board (6; full block + stats in `factions.md`)
The Dragon Throne (Phillipe) · The Lord Protector's Party (Almeric) · The Mauressac Claim (Marie) · The Worm-Cult of Vael · The Arrival (the New Yorkers) · Mishar's Hand (Amiya). *Counts are Characters whose levies are Assets under the claimant they back; promote a count to a full faction if play makes them central.*

## Named NPCs (Auragne)
- **Boy-King Phillipe** — dragon-touched child, true heir — to survive and truly reign (a pawn for now).
- **Lord Protector Almeric** — regent-uncle, holds Aurholt & the boy — to make the regency permanent / supplant the line.
- **Countess Marie of Mauressac** — guild-rich count-claimant — to take the Dragon Throne outright.
- **Counts Heinrich, Deidre, Carloman, Gruith, Qasim, Chilperic** — the ealdormen (see shires) — each to come out of the war richer, safer, or crowned.
- **Vörniss, the Crowned Worm** — dragon-ancestor of the line, dreaming in Old Vael — Domination; indifferent until woken.
- **Despot Amiya of Mishar** *(off-stage)* — foreign queen buying counts — to vassalize Auragne while it bleeds.
