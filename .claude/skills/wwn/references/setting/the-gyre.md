# The Gyre — the default starting region

> Use this when: starting a campaign on the default map, or picking where to drop the PC. One tight paragraph per nation — situation, problems, hooks, name flavor — then why the Gyre is a good on-ramp. Full detail (faiths, peoples, features) in the book at the foot.

The **Gyre** is an isolated, fractured corner of the continent of Gyaros, once unified under the immortal **Reaping King** and his **Black Brass Legion**. He has sat silent in folded **Ka-Adun** for two centuries; now six successor-states circle for hegemony amid Anak raids from the south, decaying Workings, festering arratus, and a feared new season of war. Current date: **1055 AL** (After Landing). It is grim, treasure-rich, and self-interested top to bottom.

## The six nations

**Emed-Kist** — a loose monarchy ruled from Medek by **Great King Syet Lun**, who balances grudge-ridden hereditary lords against royal-loyal Darian merchant-commoners. *Problems:* feudal lords quarrel endlessly; Darian newcomers and native Kistians distrust each other; Sarulite and Anak border raids. *Hooks:* court intrigue; a dreamed-of reconquest of lost **Emed-Dar**; fief feuds; a fallen-noble heir's claim. *Names:* Lin/Darian — Suen, Wan, Kata, Pai, family Gow, Lyak, Mak (plus Byzantine-flavored Darian elite). *Genre fit:* **court intrigue**.

**Ka-Adun** — a city that is almost a province: a vast **dead megalopolis** the Legion still builds outward forever, around the living **First City**. The Reaping King is silent; **Grand Censor Euphon** leads the strongest faction; a far-too-large impoverished nobility scrabbles for the port's tariffs. *Problems:* rebels and monsters carve statelets in the empty new city; magical disasters from poorly-kept Workings outnumber anywhere else. *Hooks:* dungeon-crawl the endless new city; beggar-prince politics; the veiled men of the Yellow Ships. *Names:* Byzantine — Arcadius, Belisarius, Zoe, Eudokia, family Komnenos, Phokas. *Genre fit:* **urban / dungeon**.

**Llaigis** — a grim, half-tamed land of wild hills, ruins, and arratu badlands where **military might is the only law**; **King Hulit of Gathis** is first among a half-dozen warlords. *Problems:* unstable rule, ambitious warlords; Anakim and arratu-monsters cross the southern border; vile cults operate freely. *Hooks:* mercenary work; plundering the Deeps of the old **Rule of Shun**; sword-and-sorcery sellsword sagas. *Names:* harsh Khalan — Goruk, Hroth, Narhak, Sarra, Tarsha; family names rare (epithets/places instead). *Genre fit:* **pulp sword-and-sorcery**.

**New Voth** — the Gyre's lone **republic**, run by senators (the rich) who deplore mind-sorcery and outlaw slavery; a gold-rush of arratu-settling and freshly-opened ruins. *Problems:* a senatorial faction wants conquest; slavers chafe at the ban; popular feeling pushes war to "civilize" the neighbors. *Hooks:* frontier reclamation; ruin-plunder once sealed by old viceroys; the losers of the gold rush get no mercy. *Names:* Gaulish — Brogimar, Domnu, Adia, Epona, family Ankyra, Gordon. *Genre fit:* **fantasy Wild West**.

**Sarul** — a nation of the **Blighted**, ruled by the Church of the **Bleeding God** under the Red, White & Black Pontiffs; brutal direct law and fearsome **Anak crusader cavalry**. *Problems:* Church corruption is a constant fight; the Anak of Emed-Dar hate them as traitors; Blighted natures strain the leash. *Hooks:* a crusader sent wandering; a secret unbeliever; a Blighted seeking refuge; baseline visitors must go cloaked. *Names:* Akkadian — Arakhu, Balasu, Belit, Shala; family names largely unknown (role/rank epithets). *Genre fit:* **grim crusade / wasteland**.

**Thur** — a fragile **guild-industrial** state living off ancient **Workings** that pipe heat, light, and motion to its factory-towns; the **Gentry** of the Great Guilds rule via corrupt elite schools. *Problems:* the schools sell rank and expulsion; the Workings are wide open to sabotage; post-war outages turn commoners against the Gentry. *Hooks:* an expelled school candidate; a Workings-maintenance mage; guild treachery; "power theft" unrest. *Names:* English — James, Edward, Mary, Gwyn, family by trade/place (Cooper, Smith, Watermaster). *Genre fit:* **steampunk / magic-tech**.

*(Surrounding the nations: the southern **Ashblight** and Anak-held **Emed-Dar**, the **Black Spine** mountains, the **Carceral Sea** (Polop-haunted), and scattered arratus, lost provinces, and Outsider enclaves — all detailed in the book.)*

## Why it's a good on-ramp
- **Self-contained and ready:** six contrasting nations, each pre-loaded with rulers, grudges, problems, and hooks — drop the PC anywhere and conflict is at hand.
- **Every campaign flavor has a home** (the genre-fit tags above), so the player picks the tone and you start there.
- **Built for lazy worldgen:** detail only the starting nation; the open southern border, the arratus, and the wider Atlas are generated on demand as play travels (`bridge/setting-canon.md` → frontier).
- **The clock is ticking** — a silent god-king, circling successor-states, quiet-but-watched Anakim — so the world reacts and pressures on its own without the GM forcing it.
