# The Latter Earth — setting overview

> Use this when: you want the shape of the world in one read — what kind of place it is, what the Legacy/Deeps/Outsiders/Legates/Iterums are, and what that means at the table. Pointers into the book at the foot.

## What it is
The **Latter Earth** is the unimaginably far future of our own world — old past reckoning. Uncounted godlike empires have risen and crumbled to dust; their ruins, relics, and monsters litter every land. It is **post-apocalyptic sword-and-sorcery**: no science, no progress, no rescue. The future brings no improvement, only the dangerous present and the treasure-laden, lethal past. Adventurers are a recognized, disposable social role — the mad few who dare the wilds and the dead for gold and power.

## History in brief
The deep past is a stack of fallen ages with no true beginning. The **Outsiders** — innumerable alien species — once ruled humanity from the **Deeps**, breeding, "improving," and consuming their charges. When their rule finally collapsed, humans rose from the Deeps led by **sorcerer-kings**, slaughtered the remnant Outsiders, and founded the nations that fell and rose in turn ever since. Records run back a few thousand years at best; everything older is rumor, ruin, and the strange.

## The load-bearing facts
- **The Legacy** — the world's decaying source-code, the substrate all reality runs on. Its decay makes physical law **conditional, temporary, special-cased**; spells, Workings, and even chemistry can't be trusted across ages. This is *why* there's no science, no progress, and why a hundred miles of monster-haunted wilderness can sit a few days from a capital — nations simply can't secure land beyond their reach.
- **The Deeps** — vast buried megastructures the Outsiders dug; the classic dungeon. New ones surface constantly; living cities are built atop dead buried ones.
- **Outsiders** — the former alien overlords, now hunted, bitter remnants. **Genuinely alien** in motive, not cartoon-evil: the Jikegida reckon humans mindless meat-machines; the Polop eat people as a statement of supremacy; the Tuhulot ("folded men") strain to break an ancient seal and return. Most are trapped here forever and hate humanity for what it did, is, or might become.
- **Legates / sorcerer-kings** — superhuman, near-immortal rulers descended from (or being) the Deep-born conquerors. Their personal power dwarfs any commoner; in the Gyre, the immortal **Reaping King** is the archetype.
- **Arratus** — xenoformed wastelands the Outsiders left: alien atmospheres, mutant life, exceptionally well-preserved ruins. They can spread on their own until the Legacy or ancient markers halt them.
- **The Blighted** — cursed human-descended peoples warped by old sorcery; the **Anakim** are the warborn, hate-driven Blighted of the wastes.
- **Iterums** — "echoed worlds," self-contained splinter-realities the Legacy can spin off (a room to a whole Earth). The default world is one Iterum, coded **108 Triumphant Void**. Practical upshot: they're the in-world reason any GM can rewrite, add, or remove anything without "breaking canon," and they double as planes for summoning and as impossible dungeon-sites.

## At the table
- Wonder and horror together: the treasure is real, and so is its price.
- The world is **indifferent and lethal**; it owes nothing and punishes passivity (engine Creed). Stakes: death, maiming, ruin, exile — permanent.
- Keep the ancient dead **strange**. When the oracle asks "why," reach for the Legacy, the Outsiders, a sorcerer-king, or an Iterum before anything modern.
