# Resolution — skills, checks, advancement

> Use this when: a non-combat action needs a roll, or you need the skill list, opposed/aided checks, or how PCs advance.

**Roll via `dice.py roll 2d6+<mod>` and show it.**

## The skill check
`2d6 + skill level + best attribute mod` vs **difficulty**:

| Diff | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 6 | Tricky for a layman; below this, no roll |
| 8 | Real challenge a pro still beats more often than not |
| 10 | Only a skilled expert; even they may fail |
| 12 | Only a master is reliable |
| 14+ | Even a master will probably fail |

- **Untrained** (no level-0 in the skill): −1, and some esoteric skills can't be attempted at all.
- **Outcome:** meet-or-beat = success. Failure = can't do it *or* success at a cost/complication (GM decides).
- **Modifiers:** circumstances/tools cap at **±2** total. A peripheral but plausible skill raises difficulty by **+2**.
- **Don't call for a check** when success is routine for the PC's background, or when neither outcome is interesting.

## Aiding
A helper describes their aid and rolls a relevant skill vs the same difficulty; success grants the acting PC **+1** (max +1 total, regardless of helpers).

## Opposed checks
All parties roll relevant `2d6 + mod`; **highest wins, ties to the PC**. An NPC adds its skill bonus when its role fits; otherwise rolls flat 2d6. GM picks the most applicable skill/attribute (e.g. Dex/Sneak vs Wis/Notice).

## The skill list (rated 0–4)
Administer · Connect · Convince · Craft · Exert · Heal · Know · Lead · Magic · Notice · Perform · Pray · Punch · Ride · Sail · Shoot · Sneak · Stab · Survive · Trade · Work. *(Combat skills: Stab, Shoot, Punch. Magic gates Arts/spells.)*

## Time
**Round** = 6 s (combat). **Turn** = 10 min (exploration; light, encounters). **Scene** = one activity/place; most effect durations are "1 scene."

## Advancement (summary)
PCs gain XP for adventures/goals; each level raises attack bonus (class-based), HP, save targets (16 − level − attr), skill points, and grants foci at set levels. Past 10th, gains taper. Full thresholds & per-level benefits: `book/Worlds-Without-Number-Deluxe/04-The-Rules-of-the-Game.md` (L694–798).
