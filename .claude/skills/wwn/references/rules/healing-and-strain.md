# Injury, Healing & System Strain

> Use this when: a character hits 0 HP, someone applies first aid, or you track rest, Frailty, or System Strain.

## Mortal Wounds (0 HP)
- Unnamed NPC/creature → **dead**. PC or named NPC → **Mortally Wounded**: helpless, and **dies at the end of the 6th round** after going down — or **instantly on any further damage**.
- **Stabilize:** an ally spends a Main Action, **Int/Heal or Dex/Heal** vs **8 + full rounds elapsed** (+2 with no implements). One attempt per round; others may aid; retry next round.
- Once stabilized → recovers **1 HP after 10 min**, can act, but is **Frail** until a week's bed rest. **Frail**: can't heal naturally and **dies instantly if dropped to 0 again.** A physician may remove Frailty with 1 hr + a diff-10 skill check (one chance).

## First aid
A healer spends a few minutes → restores **1d6 + Heal skill** HP (−1 if untrained). Each application adds **1 System Strain** to the patient; repeatable while Strain allows. Restores HP to a Frail PC but doesn't remove Frail.

## System Strain
- **Max = Constitution score.** At max, the body rejects further forced healing/exertion (no benefit).
- Lost at **1 point per night** of good sleep. Added by first aid and many magical effects. Privation/hunger and some poisons also add Strain; forced above max → collapse and death in minutes.

## Rest & natural healing
- A night's rest (fed, decent sleep): recover **level (or HD) in HP** and **−1 System Strain**. Frail creatures recover neither.

## Magical healing
Any amount of magical healing **removes Mortally Wounded and Frail** instantly and lets the character rise. (A downed PC given 3 HP from a potion gets up next turn, no Frailty.) Healers' Arts, Necromantic vitality, elixirs, and artifacts qualify. Heal skill tending the sick grants **+2×Heal** (or +1 at Heal-0) to their saves.
